For consultants and their clients in Jaipur: plots, schemes, circle rates, feasibility

The playbook.

Six questions people in Jaipur's property trade ask, each worked through with every sum shown.

Each case starts from a question a client asks. I work the sum one step at a time, say what it does and does not tell you, and end with three things to do this week. Inputs I chose only to show the arithmetic are marked "example"; every other number is one I have checked twice, with its limit beside it.

What are you doing?

  • 01What is this residential plot in a JDA scheme worth?A value from one same-scheme comparable, adjusted for size, given as a range rather than one figure.ValuingRead
  • 02What plot sizes should a commercial scheme sell?How much more per sq m small commercial plots fetch than large ones, and why the residential rule does not carry over.Pricing · Buying landRead
  • 03The circle rate was revised. What changes in my cost line?How two 2026 revisions stack up in one office, what that does to the duty base and the cost of buying, and why one city percentage will not do.Due diligenceRead
  • 04Which price do I quote in a feasibility report?Which published price answers which question, how far apart their growth figures are, and the buyer's cost to add on top.Valuing · Due diligenceRead
  • 05JDA's auctions fell sharply. Is land softening in my corridor?What a fall in JDA's auction numbers does and does not say about land prices, read zone by zone.Timing · Buying landRead
  • 06Should my client buy at a scheme's launch, or wait?Whether plots in a scheme's first year cleared cheaper than later, what a scheme still selling in its second year signals, and whether any month is cheaper.Timing · Buying landRead

Ground rulesJDA's auctions are one seller's plots in its own schemes, not Jaipur's land market. Circle rates are minimum values for stamp duty, not prices. Nothing here says where prices go next.
