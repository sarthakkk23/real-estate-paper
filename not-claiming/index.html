The plot rate estimator

What I would like to say

Here is a calculator that tells you what a plot should fetch at JDA's auctions, from its zone, year, use, size and corner.

Why I am not saying it yet

I tested it the hard way. I hid 2026 from the sums and made it guess every 2026 plot. Its typical miss was 31.2%. A simple rule of thumb, the middle price of the same scheme and use over the year before, missed by a typical 18.4% on the same 276 auctions. On 2025 the calculator won (22.6% against 27.7%), but a tool that loses on the latest year is not ready for your file.

What would settle it

Beating the rule of thumb on a year it has not seen, with its own measured band. Until then, use the comparable adjuster with a rate from the same scheme.

The same schemes fetched far more after mid-2022

What I would like to say

Something changed in the second half of 2022, and the same schemes started fetching far more.

Why I am not saying it yet

Prices did step up then: comparing each scheme with itself, land rose +43.3% from April-June to October-December 2022, and stayed up. But in the schemes that sold in both halves of the year (607 auctions in 26 schemes), the second half was +30.4% above the first if you ignore the steady rise, and only +9.3% once you allow for it, with a likely range of −6.1% to +27.2%: it could be no jump at all. The plots sold in each half also differed. I describe the step and claim no cause.

What would settle it

JDA's starting (reserve) prices, and more resales of the same plots.

How far JDA's auctions clear above the circle rate

What I would like to say

JDA's plots sell for so many times the circle rate.

Why I am not saying it yet

The multiple moves with which colony's rate is matched to which scheme, and with the date the rate was in force. Most matches sit in one corner of the city, along one road scheme. So I give the direction only: JDA's residential auctions clear well above the residential circle rate, and commercial auctions much closer to it.

What would settle it

Dated rate histories for the 9 offices I have not read, and JDA's reserve prices.

The extra paid for commercial land, zone by zone

What I would like to say

The extra a commercial plot fetches over a residential one is smallest in one zone and largest in another.

Why I am not saying it yet

Each zone rests on only a handful of schemes that sold both uses, and the order of the zones has not been tested on recent years alone. The city-wide figure is on the findings; the zone ranking is not.

What would settle it

The same test run on 2023-2026 alone, and more schemes selling both uses.

Zone 12, north of Ajmer Road

What I would like to say

Land in zone 12 rose more than in any other JDA zone.

Why I am not saying it yet

Land in zone 12 appears to have risen even more than zone 9, but no scheme there sold in both periods, so I cannot compare like with like. JDA prints its large scheme there under zone 12 wherever it prints a zone, so the zone itself is right.

What would settle it

A scheme in zone 12 that sells in both periods.

The rest of Rajasthan's jump in plotted projects

What I would like to say

Outside Jaipur, plotted projects registering with RERA jumped in 2025 because of a rule change.

Why I am not saying it yet

Registrations outside Jaipur district went from 276 in 2024 to 670 in 2025. There is a documented candidate: the Urban Development and Housing department's circular of 30 December 2024, which lets urban bodies issue title papers (pattas) only for RERA-registered projects. But I have not shown that it caused the rise, or how much. A new numbering, projects split into phases, or a data move would look the same in these counts. Counts are projects, not homes.

What would settle it

The registration months of the 2024-25 plotted projects, to see whether the step starts in January 2025.

RERA registrations in 2026

What I would like to say

Fewer projects registered in Jaipur in 2026 than in 2025.

Why I am not saying it yet

So far 277 projects have registered in 2026, against 455 in all of 2025. The year is not over, so the two are not yet comparable.

What would settle it

A reading after the year ends, in January.

Returned plots fetch more later

What I would like to say

Plots that come back to auction later sell above their neighbours.

Why I am not saying it yet

In my files they do, but I do not know why. They may have better frontage or a better spot in the scheme, and the auction list does not say.

What would settle it

JDA's records of cancelled allotments and why each plot came back.

Prices after inflation

What I would like to say

Here are JDA's land prices, and Jaipur's circle rates, after allowing for rising prices in general.

Why I am not saying it yet

The general price index I would use for 2026 is still a placeholder, the methods do not yet agree, and the circle-rate series is adjusted only to December 2025 using price releases I have not re-read at source. Only 3 offices have dated rate histories back to 2014 for more than one colony.

What would settle it

The 2026 consumer price index, read at source, and agreement across methods.

Did offices with faster-rising JDA land get bigger July steps?

What I would like to say

Offices where JDA's land rose fastest got the biggest circle-rate rise in July 2026.

Why I am not saying it yet

The pattern rests on a handful of offices, too few to tell it from chance, and it did not pass the strict bar for many tests.

What would settle it

Dated histories for more offices, and a test that passes the strict bar.

Lower stamp duty for some buyers

What I would like to say

Joint buyers, some flats and senior citizens pay less stamp duty.

Why I am not saying it yet

I have read only summaries of these concessions, not the notifications. The cost calculator uses only the rates I read in the notifications themselves.

What would settle it

Opening the notifications.

Jaipur's share of the state's sale deeds

What I would like to say

Jaipur's share of Rajasthan's registered sale deeds jumped after 2022, and dearer deeds took a bigger share.

Why I am not saying it yet

The dashboard changed how it sorts documents twice. The statewide "other" category went from 20.8% of documents in July 2021 to 51.4% in December 2021, and the types changed again in February to April 2024. Newly reporting offices explain little of Jaipur's step: 2.8% of its documents in July 2023 (791 of 28,577). The value bands move with circle rates, so they are not a clean measure of dearer deals.

What would settle it

Sale deeds by sub-registrar office for August to December 2022, exported by hand from the dashboard.

Bank auctions of homes and plots

What I would like to say

Here is how much distressed property banks are selling in Jaipur.

Why I am not saying it yet

One day's counts from the banks' auction portal are not a series. The saved listings named borrowers, so they were deleted, and today's counts rest on a single reading.

What would settle it

Weekly readings over some months, with how each auction ended. Never a listing.

Other cities' authority auctions

What I would like to say

Jaipur's JDA rates compared with Indore's or Lucknow's.

Why I am not saying it yet

The results I found from other authorities are not measured the same way as JDA's, so they do not compare.

What would settle it

Comparable auction results from another authority.

Dropped, and why

These are not waiting for data. I have decided not to say them.

  • "Land rose three times as fast as homes." The multiple depends on the method. Dividing the land rise by the bank-valuation rise gives 2.5 to 5.8 times across the fair land methods, and 1.2 times on a simple middle price. The two also measure different things: winning bids for bare plots from one seller, and banks' valuations of homes. So I always give two numbers and never their ratio.
  • A reading about how many bids JDA's auctions draw. Dropped: the auction results page cannot show it fairly.
  • "A Jaipur circle-rate index." Before 2026 I have one office's history, and the steps differ a great deal by office. A single city figure would hide exactly what matters. I show each office's steps instead.

NeverAnd never, on this site: a forecast of prices, a "which area next" ranking (the finding that nothing predicts next year's zone price is the evidence against one), any figure for one plot or one colony, or a developer named.
