Tools

Tools for a live file.

Each tool answers one question you meet on a live file. It does arithmetic on your own figures with rates I have checked, and under every result says how sure it is, what it leaves out and where the figures come from. None of them forecasts a price.

  • All-in cost of a purchaseWhat will the registry cost on top of the price? Stamp duty, surcharges and registration fee on a value you type, by who is buying.Open the tool: All-in cost of a purchase
  • JDA comparable adjusterI have a JDA rate for one plot. What does it say about another plot of a different size, or on a corner? Carry a rate you have from a JDA scheme to a plot of another size or corner, in the same scheme and year, with how far it can be off.Open the tool: JDA comparable adjuster
  • Stamp-duty base from a circle rateOn what value will stamp duty be charged: the price, or the circle-rate value? Turn the circle rate you look up for your colony into the plot's circle-rate value, and see which of it and the price duty is charged on.Open the tool: Stamp-duty base from a circle rate
  • Circle-rate office comparatorHow do the registration offices compare on circle rates, and how did the July 2026 revision land in each? Pick one or two registration offices: the date of their 2026 revision, how far rates rose, and how their tables are built. No rupee levels.Open the tool: Circle-rate office comparator
  • Which price?The published figures for Jaipur's prices disagree. Which one answers my question? The published measures of Jaipur's prices side by side, for one window at a time: what each measures, its period and its source.Open the tool: Which price?
  • Busy and quiet months at the registryIs this month's count of sale deeds high, or is it just the season? How many more, or fewer, sale deeds a month usually sees than April, so you can compare two months fairly.Open the tool: Busy and quiet months at the registry
  • JDA plot rate estimatorNot yet

    What would a plot like this fetch at JDA's auctions, in this zone and year?

    I built a calculator that estimates what a plot would fetch at JDA's auctions from its zone, year, use, size and corner, and I am holding it back. To test it I hid the latest year, 2026, from the sums, made it guess each 2026 auction, then compared. It was off by a typical 31.2%. A simple rule of thumb, the typical rate of the same scheme and use over the year before, was off by only 18.4% on the same auctions. On 2025 the calculator did better than the rule of thumb (22.6% against 27.7%), but a tool that loses on the latest year is not ready for your file. Until it wins, use the comparable adjuster with a rate from the same scheme.

    How this was tested (for analysts)
    • Hidden-year test: the calculator was fitted without 2026, then asked to price each of the 338 auctions of 2026, and compared with what they fetched (out-of-sample holdout, median absolute percentage error).
    • The rule of thumb priced the 276 of those auctions that had a sale in the same scheme and use in the year before (benchmark: trailing same scheme-use median).
    • The same test run on 2025 (fitted to 2003-2024) is the one the calculator passed (second holdout year).

These are not legal or tax advice. At the registry, the registering office's own figure is the one that counts.
