For analysts

How this was tested (for analysts)
  • Each July step is the typical change across the colony rows I sampled in that office, with the middle half shown beside it so you see how much offices vary inside (median and interquartile range of sampled colony-categories).
  • The spread inside an office compares the rows near the top with those near the bottom, so one odd row cannot drive it (ratio of the ninetieth to the tenth percentile).
  • An office's level is a ratio to the typical office, never a rupee figure (office median over the median of the office medians).
  • A second, separate script recomputes every figure here from the same table and must agree (independent cross-check).
