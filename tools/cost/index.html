Tool · All-in cost of a purchase

What will the registry cost on top of the price?

Use this when a buyer asks what he will pay at the registry on top of the price, before he signs.

The price, or the circle-rate value if that is higher: duty is charged on whichever is higher. Work out the circle-rate value.Read as ₹50 lakh (₹50,00,000).
Typed in
Who is buying

Stamp duty, surcharges and registration fee on this purchase come to ₹4.49 lakh (₹4,49,000), 8.98% of the value.

How sure

No range here: this is arithmetic on rates the state has notified, not an estimate. It is as current as my last reading of the notifications, in November 2026.

Limit

Worked from the Finance Department's notifications as I read them in November 2026; a later change has not been ruled out. Some concessions are reported, for a couple buying together and for some flats, that I have not confirmed: they would lower the total and are left out. Lease, regularisation and loan documents are charged differently and are not covered.

Source

Rajasthan Finance Department notifications, each named beside its line in the table. Read in November 2026.

These are not legal or tax advice. At the registry, the registering office's own figure is the one that counts.

For analysts

How this was tested (for analysts)
  • I read every rate from the words of its notification, and a second, separate script reads them again and must agree (rates parsed from the notification text).
  • On ₹50 lakh the page gives ₹4,49,000 for a man, ₹3,82,500 for a woman, ₹3,16,000 for a woman of the SC, ST or BPL categories and ₹3,82,500 for a person with benchmark disabilities; on ₹10 lakh it gives ₹88,000 for a man. The page's own sums are run on these cases and must match a second, separately written version to the rupee.
  • Empty, zero, negative and non-number inputs give a plain message and no figure (input guard tests).
