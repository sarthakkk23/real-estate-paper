Tool · Which price?

The published figures for Jaipur's prices disagree. Which one answers my question?

Use this when a client quotes "the price went up this much" and you need to know which price he means, or which one to put in a report.

Period

Left out on purpose

These measures exist but are not shown, and why:

  • A circle-rate index: built from three offices only, and circle rates move by government decision, not by sales.
  • Registration revenue per sale deed, 2023 to 2024: the state changed how deed types are counted in 2024, so the two years do not compare.
  • Average ticket size from PropEquity's totals: it would be my own arithmetic on rounded published figures.

For analysts

How this was tested (for analysts)
  • No sums here: each figure is the publisher's own, or worked out from the publisher's series where marked, and a second, separate script works those out again and must agree (independent cross-check).
  • The JDA land index compares each plot only with plots in the same scheme sold in other years, allowing for use, size and corner (repeat-scheme hedonic index, like-for-like).
  • Measures that could not be checked or that cross a change in definitions are left out, with the reason (held list).
