Why I'm doing this.

I'm Sarthak Oberoi, a data analyst in Jaipur. Jaipur is called a plot city, but the price figures usually quoted for it describe homes, not land. I wanted to see what the public records say about land, put the figures in one place, and say plainly where each one stops.

This is the third of the Pink City Papers, after cafés and jewellery: the same idea each time. Read the public records about one Jaipur trade carefully, check every number twice, and write it so that someone who runs a business in it can use it on a Monday morning.

It is free and independent. Nothing here is for sale, nobody pays for it, and no business pays to appear in it.

The house rules.

  • Totals only.Nothing for one plot, one deed, one project or one colony, and no rupee rate from the circle-rate portal.
  • Praise, never blame.No authority, firm, area or community is criticised. A business is named only to credit it, with a source.
  • No forecast.No price prediction and no "which area next".
  • Every number checked.Each figure is worked out twice, by programs written apart, and its limit sits beside it.

What would you need to see to trust this?

You know where this falls short better than I do. Tell me what you would want measured, which record you would want read, or where a number here looks wrong. I correct mistakes and note the change.
