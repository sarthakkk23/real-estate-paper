How to use them

The first row of each file names its columns. Where a file gives a likely range, its low and high columns mark a range that holds the true figure 95% of the time. Figures from JDA's auctions describe one seller's plots, not the whole market.

What is not here, and why

Nothing for one plot, one deed, one project or one colony. The auction list names each plot and the circle-rate tables name each colony. Totals are what a valuation needs, and they cannot be traced back to a person.

No rupee circle rate. The circle-rate portal's terms bar copying its rates, so the tables here hold only ratios, steps and shares by office. Until the office that publishes them allows reuse, that is all I show.

No list of RERA projects. I read counts from the public search page; I did not collect the projects.

My working files stay with me: the cleaned auction list, the matched records and my notes. If a table you need is missing, write to me and say what it is for.
