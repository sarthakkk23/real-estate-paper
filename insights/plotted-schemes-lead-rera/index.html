Lead finding · What gets registered

Plotted schemes now lead Jaipur's new RERA registrations, on a long trend.

Use this when you write the note on what to build on a Jaipur site: plotted colony or apartments.

6 in 10

New RERA projects in Jaipur district that were plotted schemes in 2025, up from about 3 in 10 in 2018

To one decimal place: 57.4% in 2025 (261 of 455) and 32.4% in 2018 (36 of 111).

RERA (the state's register of real estate projects) counts projects, not homes or area. Most of the rise came before the December 2024 rule change.

In 2018, about 3 in 10 new RERA projects in Jaipur district were plotted schemes. In 2025 it was about 6 in 10. This is a long trend since 2018, not a one-year jump: the trend up to 2024 alone points to about 53% for 2025, close to what happened.

What it means for a consultantThe move from apartments to plots in Jaipur did not start with one rule change at the end of 2024. You can put it in a highest-and-best-use note as a long shift in what gets registered, not as a one-year spike.

What it means for the clientA developer choosing between a tower and a plotted layout is choosing the product that has gained ground almost every year since 2018. A buyer will see more plotted projects on the register.

A second, separate source says the sameLiases Foras counts homes sold, not projects registered, so it is a separate check on the same shift. It shows plots gaining ground in what sells too, and most of that rise also came before the December 2024 rule change.

51%

Plots and villas, share of Jaipur homes sold in the quarter to September 2023, in projects tracked by Liases Foras for CREDAI; about 16% in September 2018

To one decimal place: 51.3% (1,442 of 2,813) and 15.6% (233 of 1,490).

Units sold in one firm's tracked projects, plots and villas together; the report ends in September 2023. Never added to or averaged with the RERA share.

Where it stops RERA counts projects or phases, not homes or area. A project's year is the year in its registration number. 67% of the rise in the plotted share came before the state's circular of 30 December 2024, which may still have added to 2025.

How I know
  • What I read. The counts that RERA Rajasthan's public search shows for each project type and year, read on 13 and 14 November 2026.
  • Read twice. All 20 Jaipur counts by type and year came out the same on both days.
  • How I tested "no break". I fitted the trend from 2018 to 2024 only, then asked what it expected for 2025. It expected about 53%; 2025 came in at about 57%, close enough to be chance.

Each column is one year's new projects in Jaipur district, and how many of them were plotted schemes. The share has climbed in steps since 2018. 2017 was the register's first year; I measure the trend from 2018.

How I checked it

I tried to break this finding. Each line asks one question a doubter would ask.

  • Was 2025 a sudden break? The trend fitted to 2018-2024 alone expected about 53%; 2025 came in at about 57%. A gap that small could easily be chance.
  • Did the December 2024 circular cause it? 67% of the rise in the plotted share from 2018 to 2025 had already happened by 2024. The circular may have added to 2025; it did not start the shift.
  • Did the counts move between readings? 98 of 100 counts read on both 13 and 14 November were identical, and all 20 Jaipur type-by-year counts were.
  • Is it only RERA? A separate source, Liases Foras's sales figures for CREDAI, shows the same shift in homes sold, from about 16% to about 51%. It counts different things, so I show it beside, never mixed in.
  • 2026 so far. 57.8% of the 277 projects registered in 2026, up to mid-November, are plotted.

What this does not show

  • Not homes. One tower and one small colony each count as one project. The share of homes built could be very different.
  • Not the state. This is Jaipur district. The rest of Rajasthan has its own pattern.
  • Not what sells next. It describes what was registered; it does not say what will be registered or sold later.

The tests behind this (for analysts)

For analysts: what each test asked, and what it found. The technical name is in brackets.

  • Was 2025 off the trend? The rise in the plotted share was 25.0 points from 2018 to 2025, 16.8 points of it by 2024 (67.2%). Trend fitted to 2018-2024, then 2025 compared with it: expected 53.4%, observed 57.4%, p 0.30. (Quasi-binomial trend; out-of-trend test.)
  • How sure is each year's share? Each year's share with its 95% range is in the download. (Wilson intervals.)
  • Is the RERA share stable across readings? 98 of 100 counts identical on two days. (Re-read check.)
  • Is there an independent line? Liases Foras units sold, 15.6% to 51.3%, kept separate from RERA because it counts units, not projects. (Independent source; not pooled.)
