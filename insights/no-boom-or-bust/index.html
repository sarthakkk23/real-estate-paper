Finding · What gets registered

Registrations show no Jaipur boom or bust, April 2024 to October 2026.

Use this when someone uses the number of registered sale deeds as a sign that Jaipur is booming or slumping.

+2.0%

A year: the change in Jaipur district's sale deeds, once the usual busy and quiet months are allowed for

It could be anywhere from −1.5% to +5.7%: no clear rise or fall. A short window. Deeds count every transfer, not new homes.

+1.7%

A year: the same for the rest of Rajasthan

It could be anywhere from −2.1% to +5.6%.

From April 2024 to October 2026, Jaipur's registered sale deeds moved about +2.0% a year once the seasons are allowed for, and the rest of Rajasthan about +1.7%. Both are small enough to be no change at all. There was no rush and no freeze.

What it means for a consultantDo not use registration counts as a sign of a Jaipur boom or slump in this window. They show neither.

What it means for the clientThere is no sign in the records of a rush or a freeze in registered deals.

Where it stops A short window, April 2024 to October 2026. The state changed how it classes deeds twice before this window, so earlier months are not comparable. A sale deed is any transfer of property, not only new homes.

How I know
  • What I read. The state's registration dashboard, month by month, for every district.
  • How I compared. Each month's count of sale deeds, after taking out the usual pattern of busy and quiet months, then the steady change a year.
  • How sure. Jaipur's change could be anywhere from −1.5% to +5.7% a year. Zero, no change, is inside that range. Jaipur's share of the state's deeds went from 20.0% to 23.0%, but that rise could be chance.

The tests behind this, for analysts

The change a year in sale deeds, seasons allowed for.

Both bars are small, and both ranges run from a fall to a rise: neither a boom nor a bust.

The state's registration dashboard, read 13 and 14 November 2026. Sale deeds of every kind.

How I checked it

I tried to break this finding. Each line asks one question a doubter would ask.

  • Was there a sudden jump or drop? I looked for a month where the level broke; none stood out beyond chance.
  • Did Jaipur gain on the state? Its share went from 20.0% in April 2024 to 23.0% in October 2026, but there was no steady trend; it could be chance.
  • Is it the seasons? The usual pattern of months was taken out first, so a busy May does not pass for a boom.

What this does not show

  • Not new homes. A sale deed records any transfer, including resales and plots.
  • Not prices. This counts deeds, not what they were worth.
  • Not before April 2024. Changes in how deeds are classed make earlier months not comparable.

The tests behind this (for analysts)

For analysts: what each test asked, and what it found. The technical name is in brackets.

  • Is there a trend? Jaipur +2.0% a year (95% range −1.5% to +5.7%); rest of the state +1.7% (−2.1% to +5.6%). (Log-linear trend with month effects on seasonally comparable months.)
  • Was there a break? No break beyond chance. (Structural break test.)
  • Did Jaipur's share trend? No trend beyond chance. (Trend test on the monthly share.)
