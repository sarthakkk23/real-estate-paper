Finding · What gets registered

"Completed" on RERA records a filing, not a handover.

Use this when you write a due-diligence note on a project and the register shows it as "Completed".

57%

Of Jaipur projects registered with RERA in 2024 were already marked "Completed" by November 2026, 23 to 34 months after registering

To one decimal place: 57.3%, that is exactly 149 of 260 projects.

Projects, not homes. The register's "Completed" status records what has been filed; it is not a record that homes were handed over. Statuses change day to day.

52%

The share for projects registered in 2017

To one decimal place: 52.1%, that is exactly 146 of 280 projects.

The same label, read the same day.

About 6 in 10 Jaipur projects registered with RERA in 2024 were already marked "Completed" by November 2026, 23 to 34 months after they registered. That is about the same share as for projects registered in 2017. The register's "Completed" status records what has been filed; it is not a record that homes were handed over.

What it means for a consultantIn a lender's or buyer's due-diligence note, do not count the register's "Completed" status as handover. Ask for the documents that show it.

What it means for the clientFor handover, a buyer should ask for the occupancy certificate.

Where it stops RERA counts projects, not homes. The register's "Completed" status records what has been filed; it is not a record that homes were handed over. Statuses change from day to day.

How I know
  • What I read. RERA Rajasthan's public search: RERA is the state's register where builders file each project. I counted Jaipur projects by the year they registered and by the status the register shows.
  • How I compared. The share marked "Completed" for each year of registration, read on the same day in November 2026.
  • How sure. It is the whole register, not a sample, and I read it twice. The shares for the years between 2018 and 2024 show no steady rise or fall.

How I checked it

I tried to break this finding. Each line asks one question a doubter would ask.

  • Is 2024 an odd year? No: the shares for each year from 2018 to 2024 show no steady trend, so younger projects are marked "Completed" about as often as older ones.
  • What does the status follow? On the one project page I read, the status matched the declared completion date.
  • Is it a misreading? The search was read twice with the same counts.

What this does not show

  • Not handover. It is the register's status, not an inspection.
  • Not any project. Nothing here is about a single project or builder.
  • Not homes. A project of many flats counts the same as a small one.

The tests behind this (for analysts)

For analysts: what each test asked, and what it found. The technical name is in brackets.

  • Is there a trend in the share by year of registration, 2018 to 2024? No: the movement could be chance. (Trend test on yearly shares.)
  • Is a sampling test needed for the two years shown? No: they are complete counts of the register. (Census count, read twice.)
