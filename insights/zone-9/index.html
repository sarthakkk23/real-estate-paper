Finding · Land at JDA's auctions

Zone 9 had the largest rise of any JDA zone I could measure fairly.

Use this when a land owner near JDA's zone 9 schemes (Jagatpura, Goner Road, the south-east Ring Road) asks what JDA's own plots there did.

185%

Rise in plot prices at JDA's auctions in zone 9, 2020-22 to 2025-26, each scheme compared only with its own earlier sales

To one decimal place: +184.6%; it could be as low as +150.5% or as high as +223.4%.

Only 4 schemes sold in both periods, and 73 auctions in 2025-26. JDA's own schemes, not a colony's value. It does not say zone 9 will keep rising.

In JDA's zone 9, plots at JDA's auctions sold in 2025-26 for about 2.8 times their 2020-22 level, each scheme compared only with its own earlier sales: a rise of about 185%. That is the largest rise of any JDA zone I could measure fairly, with enough schemes selling in both periods to compare like with like.

What it means for a consultantFor land acquisition near these schemes, this is the public record of what JDA's own plots did. It is context for a price, not a guide to the next one.

What it means for the clientA land owner near these schemes has a public reference for what JDA's own plots there fetched.

Where it stops At JDA's auctions only, in JDA's own schemes; not a colony's value. Only 4 schemes sold in both periods, and only 73 auctions came in 2025-26. Nothing in JDA's data predicts next year's zone price (see: Nothing predicts next year), so this does not say zone 9 will keep rising.

How I know
  • What I read. 758 zone 9 plot auctions from 2019 to 2026, in 46 schemes. JDA divides its area into numbered zones; zone 9 is the south-east.
  • How I compared. Each scheme only with its own sales in other years, allowing for use, size and corner. Then the average level of 2025-26 against that of 2020-22.
  • How sure. It could be as low as +150.5% or as high as +223.4%. Leaving out one scheme at a time, it stayed between +170.2% and +196.9%.

The tests behind this, for analysts

Zone 9's price level at JDA's auctions, year by year.

Each bar is one year's level, with 2021 set at 100. The steepest climb came from 2022 to 2023. The later bars rest on fewer sales: 42 auctions in 2025 and 31 in 2026.

JDA's auction results, read 13 November 2026. 2026 runs to 10 November.

How I checked it

I tried to break this finding. Each line asks one question a doubter would ask.

  • Is it one scheme? Leaving out each scheme in turn: +170.2% to +196.9%.
  • Is it the partial year 2026? Against 2025 alone: +155.3%.
  • Is it the start point? Starting from 2021-22 instead: +164.7%.
  • Is it my zone labels? On the 740 auctions where JDA printed the zone itself: +185.7%.
  • How thin are the years? 35 auctions in 2019, 307 in 2021, 42 in 2025 and 31 in 2026.

What this does not show

  • Not a forecast. A big rise in the past says nothing about the next year. I tested this for every zone (see: Nothing predicts next year).
  • Not a colony's value. These are JDA's plots in JDA's schemes.
  • Not the whole zone. Only 4 schemes sold in both periods.

The tests behind this (for analysts)

For analysts: what each test asked, and what it found. The technical name is in brackets.

  • How big is the rise? +184.6% (95% range +150.5% to +223.4%). (Same-scheme hedonic index, period averages; errors clustered by scheme.)
  • Does one scheme drive it? +170.2% to +196.9%. (Leave-one-scheme-out.)
  • How wide is each year? For 2025, 220.9 to 331.6 on the 2021 base; for 2026, 266.3 to 415.2. (Scheme bootstrap.)
