What the public records say.

Each finding is one sentence and one number, with where it stops written beside it. Pick what you are doing to see the findings that help with it.

What are you doing?

Six findings to start with.

Each says when to use it, the number, and where it stops. One click opens the full finding.

Land at JDA's auctions

Plots rose far faster than homes, 2019 to 2025.

Use this when you bring an old land value up to today, or a plot owner reads a home-price index as if it were his plot's price.

126%

Rise in plot prices at JDA's auctions, 2019 to 2025

Where it stops One government seller's new plots in its own schemes, not Jaipur's whole land market. It could be anywhere from +88.6% to +174.0%, and only 6 schemes sold plots in both years. Zone by zone instead, the rise is +96.7% to +105.0%.

Read the finding: Land against homes

Which plot

A big commercial (shop) plot fetches less per sq m; a big house plot barely does.

Use this when you price a large commercial plot, or decide what mix of plot sizes a scheme should sell.

24%

Less per sq m for a commercial (shop) plot of 300-500 sq m (359-598 sq yd) than one of 100-150 sq m (120-179 sq yd), same JDA scheme and year

Where it stops At JDA's auctions only. JDA sets its starting price per sq m, which may pull bids towards one rate.

Read the finding: Which plot size

How sure is a comparable

A comparable from the same scheme and year is good to about 14% either way.

Use this when you value a plot from a comparable sale, or a client is quoted a rate that looks far from the scheme's usual.

±14%

How close a comparable from the same JDA scheme, use and year gets you: 8 in 10 plots sold within about this much of the scheme's usual rate

Where it stops At JDA's auctions, not the open market.

Read the finding: How wide a comparable

What gets registered

Plotted schemes now lead Jaipur's new RERA registrations, on a long trend.

Use this when you write the note on what to build on a Jaipur site: plotted colony or apartments.

6 in 10

New RERA projects in Jaipur district that were plotted schemes in 2025, up from about 3 in 10 in 2018

Where it stops RERA (the state's register of real estate projects) counts projects, not homes or area. Most of the rise came before the December 2024 rule change.

Read the finding: Plotted schemes lead

Which price, and what a purchase costs

Jaipur's published prices disagree, so name the one you quote.

Use this when a feasibility report or a valuation has to say which price it quotes.

−3.4% to +64.6%

How much Jaipur's published price figures moved, each over its own twelve months, all within July 2022 to December 2024: from builders' quoted prices to new launch prices

Where it stops Each publisher's twelve months are different, so the periods do not match exactly. No figure is ranked as right: each answers a different question.

Read the finding: Which price

Land at JDA's auctions

The simple middle price hides about half the rise.

Use this when someone quotes the typical auction price, the middle one, as what land did.

47%

Rise in the simple middle price of JDA's winning bids, 2019 to 2025: half sold above it, half below

Where it stops It follows where JDA sold. Comparing like with like, land rose about 97% zone by zone (+96.7%) and about 126% scheme by scheme (+125.8%).

Read the finding: The middle price hides the rise

Two things the data say no to.

Both are useful answers. They tell you what you can stop looking for.

Timing

Nothing in JDA's data tells you next year's price in a zone.

Use this when someone asks "which corridor next?", or a pitch names next year's winning zone from past auction prices. You can stop looking for that answer in JDA's auction data.

No signal worked

Five signals from JDA's auctions, tested on 28 zone-years (one zone in one year): together they guessed next year's price change in a zone worse than the city's average did

Where it stops A small set, and only the signals JDA publishes. It says these signals do not work, not that nothing ever could.

Read the finding: Nothing predicts next year

Timing

There is no launch discount inside a JDA scheme.

Use this when a client asks whether to buy at a scheme's launch or wait. You can stop looking for a launch discount at JDA's auctions: inside a scheme, the timing made no difference.

Less than 1%

The difference between plots sold in a scheme's first year and in its second year: they sold for about the same, each scheme compared only with itself

Where it stops At JDA's auctions only. It assumes a scheme's prices stop changing with its age after three years, and it covers schemes that started from 2007.

Read the finding: No launch discount

How every number was testedEvery number on this site was worked out twice, by two separate programs, and put through the hardest tests I know: leaving parts of the data out, re-running the sums on reshuffled data, hiding a year and making the sums guess it. Each finding's page says in plain words what the tests found, and lists them by name at the end, for analysts.
